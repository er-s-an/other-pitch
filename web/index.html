How it works

  1. Decide. Each play freezes at one decision. You pick a pass or a shot.
  2. See your branch. Your choice plays first: ball, receiver and the key defender move under a fixed, public rule set.
  3. Compare. Switch to the recorded action or another option, all from the same moment and the same 8-second window.
  4. Change one condition. In play one, move one defender into the passing lane and watch the numbers and the explanation follow.

About this model

What the numbers mean

Estimated pass completion
Internal estimate that the first pass reaches the intended team-mate. Shown as a percentage.
Estimated possession risk
Share of the outcome tree where the ball is lost before the window ends or the first shot.
Simulated shot quality
Synthetic 0–100 score at the moment of the shot. Not calibrated on real matches.
Opportunity quality
Probability-weighted shot quality of the first legal shot within 8 seconds, shown out of 100. It is not a chance of scoring.

Teams, players and positions are invented. Coefficients are transparent heuristics, not fitted to real data. Each animation is one representative simulated path; the numbers come from the outcome tree.

How the play was made

Producer generation, run before publishing: a Microsoft Agent Framework workflow (Moment Director → Replay Analyst → Fan Editor → QA, with a conditional revise loop) calls this project's MCP server over stdio for snapshots, legal actions, branch simulations and evidence. Code does the arithmetic; QA checks every number's subject, metric, unit and window. The site serves the validated, immutable result.
